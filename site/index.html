Community review pilot

Help check how Sanskrit sentences in the project's data are split at sandhi junctions. The worksheet has 60 short items and takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

Open the worksheet

Your answers are saved in your browser as you go, so please finish on the same device and browser. When you are done, click Hand in: the worksheet seals your answers, and you paste them into a short GitHub form (a free account is needed). Nobody can read sealed answers until the round closes, then scoring and the leaderboard run automatically. Please work alone until then.

Read the reviewer guide first. Sentences come from the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (Krishna et al. 2017, CC BY 4.0).
